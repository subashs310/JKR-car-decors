About Our Shop

A local, family-run car accessories and decoration shop built on honest work and genuine parts.

Our Story

Built Around Every Car That Comes Through Our Doors

JKR Car Decors & Accessories started in 2010 and has grown into a full-service car styling and installation shop. What hasn't changed is the way we work: every car gets the same care, every accessory is genuine, and every fitting is done by hand, in-house, by people who know what they're doing.

Today we stock over 500 products across every category — from seat covers and lighting to full audio and camera systems — and our technicians install all of it on-site.

Our Mission

To make quality car accessories and professional installation accessible, affordable and honest — for every car, every budget.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted car styling and accessories shop in the region, known for genuine parts and workmanship that lasts.

Our Commitment

Every product we sell is genuine and tested. Every installation is carried out by an experienced technician — no shortcuts.
